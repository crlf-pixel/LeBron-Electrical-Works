About us

A lifetime in the electrical trade.

LeBron Electrical Works is a local Omaha electrical company with family roots reaching back to 1906. Roche continues that tradition today as a Master Electrician, serving residential, commercial, and industrial customers throughout Omaha and the surrounding area.

Electrical work is personal when your family name is on the business. The focus is straightforward: honest communication, practical problem-solving, careful workmanship, and treating every customer’s property with respect.
